== Mutation ratio (the slide's name; also called mutation score)
\text{Mutation Ratio} = \frac{\#\text{Killed}}{\#\text{Total} - \#\text{Equivalent}}
? Small deliberate bugs (mutants) are planted in the code. A mutant is "killed" when a test fails because of it.
? The score is the share of bugs your tests noticed.
? Equivalent mutants behave exactly like the original, so no test could catch them and they are left out.
> 20 mutants, 3 equivalent, 14 killed.
= \var{ratio} = \frac{14}{20 - 3} = 0.82
== Related slide formulas
\text{Test Pass Rate} = \frac{\#\text{Passed tests}}{\#\text{Total tests}}
\text{Unit-level coverage} = \frac{\#\text{Units tested at least once}}{\#\text{Total units}}
~ Slide example: pass rate 25 / 26 = about 0.96.
? Pass rate: the share of tests that pass. Coverage: the share of units that any test touches.
? Neither shows the tests are good: a test can run code and check nothing.
> 25 of 26 tests pass (0.96), and 8 of 10 units are tested (0.8).
== Weighted total
\text{total} = \sum \text{weight} \times \text{score}
? Multiply each score by how much that criterion matters, then add.
> Scores 8 and 6 with weights 0.7 and 0.3 give 7.4.
